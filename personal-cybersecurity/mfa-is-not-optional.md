# MFA Is Not Optional; But One Method Is Not Enough
A password is no longer enough to protect an important account.

Passwords can be stolen, but even if an attacker has your password, they may still be stopped because they do not have your phone, authenticator app, security key, fingerprint, or passkey.

That extra step can be the difference between a stolen password and a stolen account.

## What Counts as MFA?
MFA requires more than one type of proof:

- Something you know: a password or PIN
- Something you have: a phone, authenticator app, passkey, or security key
- Something you are: a fingerprint or facial scan

Not every MFA method provides the same level of protection.
