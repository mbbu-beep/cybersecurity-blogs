# 🔐 Personal Cybersecurity

### Practical security guidance for individuals, families, and everyday digital life.

Personal cybersecurity goes beyond using a strong password.

This section covers the situations that require a little more thought—protecting family accounts, securing devices, recovering from compromises, managing privacy, and making safer decisions online.

The goal is not to make your digital life complicated. It is to help you understand your risks and build realistic habits that protect your accounts, devices, money, and personal information.
