# 🛡️ Small Business Security

### Practical cybersecurity guidance for small businesses without a full IT department.

Small businesses face many of the same cybersecurity risks as larger organizations, but often with fewer people, smaller budgets, and less time to manage them.

This section focuses on realistic ways to protect business accounts, devices, employees, customer information, and daily operations without making security unnecessarily complicated.

## What You’ll Find Here

- Account and access management
- Employee onboarding and offboarding
- Password managers and MFA
- Phishing and security awareness
- Microsoft 365 and cloud security
- Incident response planning
- Vendor and third-party risk
- Security policies and documentation
- Cyber insurance readiness
- Practical controls for small teams
