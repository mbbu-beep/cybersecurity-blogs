# 📰 Security Watch

### Thoughts and analysis on evolving cybersecurity practices, identity technology, industry changes, and emerging threats.

Cybersecurity is constantly changing.

New technologies can improve security, but they can also introduce new risks, privacy concerns, and unexpected consequences. 
Industry recommendations change, attackers adapt, and the tools we rely on continue to evolve.

This section looks at those changes from a practical perspective to determine what is happening, why it matters, and how it may affect individuals, families, small businesses, and everyday technology users.

The goal is not to react to every headline. It is to take a closer look at meaningful security developments and explain what they may mean in the real world.

## What You’ll Find Here

* Cybersecurity news and industry developments
* Passwordless authentication, passkeys, and identity technology
* Changes to security tools and recommended practices
* Emerging threats, scams, and attack methods
* Privacy concerns and data-protection issues
* Security incidents and lessons learned
* New technology and its potential risks
* Commentary on where cybersecurity may be heading
